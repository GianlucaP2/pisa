**What matters**: ask only what the words can't tell you, once, the most specific question.

**How Pisa does it**
- Missing time for an event → "A che ora?". Two matches → name both. Contradiction (two times, two days) →
  "Alle 15 o alle 17?".
- Everything else: act on the most likely reading, say the detail that could be wrong (the day, the time)
  and that it can be undone.
- After the answer, act at once citing both pieces of words (["metti il dentista giovedì", "alle 15:30"]).
- Never ask what the user just said, never "vuoi che lo faccia?" for what they asked.

**Pitfalls**: asking at night "domani quale?" (skill night-domani: act and name the day); asking twice.
